—Common questions

Honest answers about what to expect

Does it hurt? +

Topical numbing is applied before and during the procedure to keep you comfortable. Most clients describe a mild scratching sensation rather than pain. Discomfort varies by treatment and individual sensitivity, but the experience is generally very manageable. Sherri will check in with you throughout to make sure you feel at ease.

How long does it last? +

Results typically last 1–3 years depending on the treatment, your skin type, lifestyle, and sun exposure. Oilier skin and more sun exposure can fade pigment more quickly. Annual touch-ups are recommended to keep the color fresh and the shape refined over time.

How long is an appointment? +

Plan for 2–3 hours for most treatments. This allows time for a thorough consultation, numbing, the procedure itself, and aftercare review. Your booking confirmation will include an estimated duration specific to your service.

What does healing look like? +

The treated area will appear bold and intense immediately after the appointment — this is completely normal and expected. Over the following 1–2 weeks, color softens as the skin heals and light flaking occurs. The true healed result becomes visible around 4–6 weeks post-appointment. A perfecting touch-up at that stage completes the final look.

How should I prepare? +

Arrive with clean skin and no makeup on the treatment area. Avoid caffeine and alcohol the day of your appointment, and skip blood thinners if your doctor agrees. For lip blush, hydrate well and consider gentle exfoliation 2–3 days before. Full preparation guidance by treatment is on the aftercare page.

Can I wear makeup afterward? +

You should avoid applying makeup directly to the treated area during the healing period, typically 7–14 days depending on the service. Makeup elsewhere on the face is generally fine. Once healing is complete, you can wear whatever you like — most clients find they need far less.

How many sessions will I need? +

Most clients receive an initial session followed by a perfecting touch-up 6–8 weeks later. The touch-up refines color, fills any areas that healed lighter or unevenly, and completes the final result. After that, annual color refreshers keep your brows, lips, or liner looking their best.

Do you offer touch-ups? +

Yes. A perfecting touch-up at 6–8 weeks after your initial session is part of the process for most treatments and is highly recommended. Annual color refreshers are also available to maintain your results and keep the pigment looking fresh year after year.

Do you work on all skin tones? +

Absolutely. Sherri has dedicated expertise in melanin-rich skin and carefully tailors pigment selection and technique to each client's unique complexion. Beautiful, natural-looking results are possible for every skin tone. Learn more about Sherri's melanin-care approach.

Who is not a candidate? +

Permanent makeup is not recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding. Certain medical conditions, medications, or active skin concerns — such as eczema or psoriasis on the treatment area — may require doctor clearance before proceeding. This is discussed during your consultation so we can make the right call together.

Is it safe? How is hygiene handled? +

Safety is non-negotiable. Single-use, sterile needles are used for every client without exception and disposed of immediately after. All surfaces and tools are sanitised to strict professional standards. Sherri follows established PMU hygiene protocols to ensure every appointment is clean and safe.

How do I book and pay? +

Appointments are booked online — just use the Book Now link and choose your service. New client rates apply to your first visit. Flexible payment plans are available so the investment fits comfortably. A deposit secures your time at booking; full payment details and terms are confirmed then.
