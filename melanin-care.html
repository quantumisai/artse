Melanin Skin Care · Mestyque

Brighter, more even-toned skin

Mestyque is a non-invasive Korean melanin-care treatment that gently lightens darkened or uneven pigmentation. No needles, no pigments, no downtime — just healthier, more radiant skin.

Mestyque · Korean melanin-care system
01What is Mestyque?

Mestyque is a skin-lightening and melanin-correction system that originates from South Korea. Using professional-grade ampoules and creams, it treats areas with excess pigmentation by supporting your skin's natural cell turnover and repairing hyperpigmentation from within.

Unlike treatments that only mask discolouration, Mestyque restores your natural skin tone by encouraging deep regeneration and targeting melanin build-up at its root.

02How it works

Degrade, expel, inhibit

Rather than bleaching the surface, Mestyque works with your skin's biology: it helps break down excess melanin, supports its natural removal as the skin renews, and helps slow new pigment from forming. The result is a brighter, more even tone that looks like healthy skin, not a cover-up.

The process of change  ·  from original skin, to degrade, to expel, to inhibit.

Many clients see a difference after a single session. With a consistent series, the improvement is gradual and long-lasting, restoring a naturally even tone rather than a temporary fix.
