Pre & Post Care

Caring for your results

Great healing is half the result. You'll receive a personalised care card at your appointment — here are the essentials, by treatment.

Brow Care Lip Blush Eyeliner & Lash Brow Lamination

01Brow Care

Microblading, combination & powder brows

Before your appointment

  • Arrive with clean skin, free of makeup
  • Avoid caffeine and alcohol the day of your appointment
  • Skip brow waxing or tinting for at least 48 hours prior
  • Avoid blood thinners if your doctor agrees (aspirin, ibuprofen, fish oil)
  • Come with your natural brow growth visible so Sherri can map your shape accurately

After your appointment

  • Keep the area clean and dry for the first 7–10 days
  • No sweating, swimming, or saunas during healing
  • Do not pick or scratch as your brows flake naturally
  • Apply the provided ointment sparingly, as directed
  • No makeup directly on the brows while healing
  • Keep out of direct sun; use SPF once healed

Brows will look bold and dark immediately after — this is normal. Color softens and settles beautifully over 4–6 weeks. A perfecting touch-up is recommended 6–8 weeks later.

02Lip Care

Lip blush

Before your appointment

  • Hydrate well in the days leading up to your appointment
  • Exfoliate gently 2–3 days before to smooth the lip surface
  • If you have a history of cold sores, speak with your doctor about antiviral treatment beforehand — lip procedures can trigger flare-ups
  • Avoid lip plumping products for at least a week prior
  • Arrive without lip makeup

After your appointment

  • Blot gently and keep the area clean
  • Keep lips moisturised with the provided balm
  • Avoid spicy, salty, or acidic foods while healing
  • Drink through a straw in the early days
  • Do not pick as lips flake — let it happen naturally
  • Skip lipstick and lip liner while healing

Color will appear intense at first and soften significantly over 1–2 weeks. The true result shows once healing is complete. A touch-up at 6–8 weeks perfects the final look.

03Eyeliner & Lash Care

Permanent eyeliner & lash enhancement

Before your appointment

  • Remove lash extensions beforehand; do not arrive with them on
  • No lash tint or lift for at least 48 hours prior
  • Arrive without eye makeup, mascara, or contacts
  • Avoid caffeine the day of your appointment to minimise eye movement
  • Bring glasses if you wear contacts — you'll need them post-appointment

After your appointment

  • Keep the area dry and clean during healing
  • No eye makeup or mascara until fully healed
  • Do not rub or touch your eyes
  • Avoid swimming, steam rooms, and saunas
  • Use the provided aftercare as directed
  • Wear sunglasses outdoors to protect from sun and dust

Some swelling and tenderness is normal for the first day or two. Your lashes may feel tender — this settles quickly. Follow care instructions closely to protect the pigment.

04Brow Lamination Care

Brow styling treatment

First 24–48 hours

  • Keep brows completely dry — no water, cleansers, or toners on the brow area
  • Avoid steam, saunas, and sweating during this window
  • Do not touch, brush, or manipulate the brows while they set
  • Sleep on your back if possible to avoid displacing the shape overnight

Ongoing care

  • Once set, brush brows into shape daily with a spoolie
  • Nourish with a brow oil or serum to keep hairs conditioned
  • Avoid oil-based products directly on the brows — they can loosen the set
  • Results typically last 6–8 weeks depending on hair type and care

Brow lamination is a styling treatment, not a permanent pigmentation service. Regular nourishing care keeps brow hairs healthy and extends the life of your results.

Healing varies person to person. Always follow the personalised instructions you're given at your appointment, and reach out with any concerns — Sherri is here to support you through the process.
