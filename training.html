Training · The Artsé Academy

Learn the craft

Hands-on permanent makeup education from a working artist with a decade of experience. Build the skills, and the confidence, to create natural, beautiful results.

Inside the studio
Where you'll learn

You won't learn in a classroom that's removed from real practice. Every course takes place inside Sherri's working studio, on the same equipment and setup she uses with clients every day. Cohorts are kept deliberately small so you receive direct, one-on-one guidance rather than watching from a distance.

The support doesn't stop at course completion. Mentorship continues after the course ends, so when real questions come up in your own studio, you have someone to ask.
