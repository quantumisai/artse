Pico Laser · Tattoo Removal
01What is Pico Laser tattoo removal?

Pico laser tattoo removal uses ultra-short pulses of laser energy measured in picoseconds — one trillionth of a second. These rapid pulses create a powerful photoacoustic effect that shatters tattoo ink into tiny particles without relying primarily on heat.

Once the ink is broken down into microscopic fragments, your body's natural immune system gradually eliminates the particles over time, causing the tattoo to fade with each treatment session.

02How Pico Laser works

From embedded ink to a fading tattoo — the science in five steps.

The tattoo fades

03Pico vs. traditional lasers

Why choose Pico over older removal lasers

Traditional Q-Switched lasers use nanosecond pulses and rely more heavily on heat to break down ink. Pico lasers deliver energy much faster, fragmenting ink more efficiently while minimizing damage to the surrounding tissue.

04How effective is it?

Among the most advanced removal treatments available

While no laser can guarantee complete removal of every tattoo, most clients experience significant fading and excellent clearance after a series of treatments. Results depend on several factors:

  • Tattoo age
  • Ink colors used
  • Tattoo size and location
  • Professional vs. amateur tattoo
  • Ink depth and density
  • Individual immune response

Most clients require multiple sessions spaced approximately 6–8 weeks apart, to allow the body time to clear the fragmented ink between visits.

09Frequently asked questions

Everything clients ask before their first Pico Laser session.

How many sessions will I need? +
Most tattoos require 6–12 sessions, although some may need fewer or more depending on the tattoo's age, colors, depth, size and location. We'll give you a realistic estimate at your consultation.
Will the tattoo disappear completely? +
Many tattoos can be removed almost entirely, but results vary based on the tattoo and individual factors. The goal is the best possible clearance for your specific ink and skin.
Does tattoo removal hurt? +
Most clients tolerate treatment well. The sensation is often compared to a rubber band snapping against the skin, and a cooling system is used to keep you comfortable throughout.
Can I remove only part of a tattoo? +
Yes. Pico laser treatments can target specific portions of a tattoo — useful for fading ahead of a cover-up, or removing just one area you no longer want.
Can Pico lasers remove cosmetic tattoos? +
Yes. Pico technology can treat certain cosmetic tattoos and unwanted pigment, such as faded brow work. A consultation is recommended to confirm suitability for your case.
