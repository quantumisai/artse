03Good to know

A few common questions about paying over time.

Will checking my eligibility affect my credit? +
No. Cherry uses a soft credit check to show your options, which does not impact your credit score. Your score is only relevant later if you choose to proceed.
What can I finance? +
Any of our services — combo, powder or microbladed brows, lip blush, lash enhancement, brow lamination, or Mestyque skin care — can be split into monthly payments.
How do I get started? +
Check your eligibility through Cherry using the button above, then book your appointment online. Prefer to talk it through? Just reach out and we'll guide you.
Are there other ways to pay? +
Of course. Cherry is simply an option for spreading the cost. New client rates apply to your first visit, and we're always happy to find what works best for you.
