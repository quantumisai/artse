07Questions, answered

Everything worth knowing before your first appointment. Still curious? Just reach out.

Does it hurt? +
Most clients find it very manageable. A topical numbing cream is applied before and during your session to keep you comfortable, and many people relax or even doze off.
How long does it last? +
Permanent makeup is long-lasting but not truly permanent. Results typically last one to three years depending on your skin, the technique and your aftercare. An annual touch-up keeps everything looking fresh.
How long is an appointment? +
Plan for around two to three hours for a first session. That includes your consultation, custom mapping and the procedure itself, so nothing ever feels rushed.
What does healing look like? +
Brows and lips look bolder for the first several days, then soften as they heal over about one to two weeks. You'll receive detailed pre-care and post-care guidance, and a touch-up perfects the final result.
Do you work on all skin tones? +
Absolutely. Pigments and technique are tuned for melanin-rich skin so your brows and lips heal true, never ashy or grey. Artistry for every shade is at the heart of what we do.
Who isn't a candidate? +
Permanent makeup isn't recommended during pregnancy or while breastfeeding, and certain skin conditions or medications may need clearance from your doctor first. When in doubt, book a consultation and we'll guide you.
How do I book and pay? +
Booking is quick and online. New client rates apply to your first visit, and flexible payment plans are available so your perfect set fits your budget.
