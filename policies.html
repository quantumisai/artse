Policies

Policies & payment

A few things to know so your appointment goes smoothly. Exact terms are confirmed when you book.

—What to expect

Simple policies, clearly stated

Booking & deposits

Securing your appointment

All appointments are booked online. A deposit is required to secure your time and is applied toward your total balance. The deposit amount is shown at the time of booking. Your spot is not held until the deposit is received.

Cancellations & rescheduling

Changes to your booking

Life happens. Please give as much notice as possible if you need to cancel or reschedule. Deposits may be non-refundable depending on the notice given. Specific terms are confirmed at the time of booking so there are no surprises.

Touch-ups

Perfecting your results

A perfecting touch-up is recommended 6–8 weeks after your initial session, once healing is complete. This appointment refines color and shape for the final result. Annual refreshers are available and encouraged to keep your look polished over time.

Patch tests & sensitivities

Sensitive skin welcome

Patch tests are available on request for clients with sensitive skin or known sensitivities. Please let us know about any allergies, skin conditions, or relevant medical history before your appointment so we can prepare accordingly.

Candidacy

Is it right for you?

Permanent makeup is not recommended during pregnancy or while breastfeeding. Certain medical conditions or medications may require written clearance from your doctor before proceeding. This is discussed during your consultation. If you have questions, reach out before booking.

Arrival & comfort

Day-of expectations

Please arrive with clean skin and no makeup on the treatment area. Numbing is applied for comfort — most clients find the experience surprisingly relaxed. Plan for the full appointment duration; rushing affects results. A calm, focused session makes all the difference.

Payment & plans

Investment that fits your life

New client rates apply to your first visit with Sherri. Flexible payment plans are available so your perfect set fits comfortably within your budget — because everyone deserves to feel confident in their skin. Details and options are shared during your consultation.
