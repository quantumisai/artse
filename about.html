Meet Sherri

Artist, owner and trainer behind Artsé Aesthetics, with more than a decade making the DMV feel and look gorgeous.

Sherri · Founder & Lead Artist
The artist

Hi beautiful, I'm Sherri — founder, lead artist, and educator behind Artsé Aesthetics.

With over a decade of experience in the beauty industry, my passion lies in helping women feel confident, empowered, and effortlessly beautiful. Every treatment is thoughtfully customized to enhance your natural features while delivering results that look refined, timeless, and uniquely you.

Whether you're visiting for permanent makeup, advanced skin treatments, or paramedical tattooing, my goal is to create an experience where artistry meets precision and every client feels seen, valued, and confident in their own skin.

As an educator and mentor, I'm equally passionate about sharing my knowledge with the next generation of artists, helping elevate the standards of our industry and preserve the artistry behind the craft.
