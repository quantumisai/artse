Meet Sherri

Artist, owner and trainer behind Artsé Aesthetics, with more than a decade making the DMV feel and look gorgeous.

Sherri · Founder & Lead Artist
The artist

Beauty is not a look — it is a feeling. Sherri has spent more than a decade making that feeling real for every person who sits in her chair.

Hello Gorgeous! I am Sherri, artist and owner of Artsé Aesthetics. My work is driven by a genuine passion for beauty in all its forms and a desire to inspire the people I work with. After more than ten years in the beauty industry, nothing is more satisfying to me than watching someone see themselves differently — bolder, more confident, more themselves.

Permanent makeup lets me meet clients where they are. Whether someone wants to simplify their morning routine, restore symmetry after hair loss, or simply wake up looking polished, I take the time to design something that fits their face and their life. Every appointment begins with a conversation, because the best results start with being heard.

As a certified trainer, I also get to carry that philosophy forward by sharing what I have learned with the next generation of artists. Passing on technique, precision and care is its own kind of artistry, and I love it just as much as the work itself.
